## 👨🏻‍💻 About me

I'm a Software Engineer with 9+ years building web applications and a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the State University of Paraíba (UEPB). For nearly 5 years at Compass UOL, a global technology consultancy, I worked on products for a client with 20M+ users, focusing on React, Next.js and TypeScript, plus Salesforce Marketing Cloud, where I built the dynamic AMPscript templates that let a whole CRM team work without depending on a developer.

One of the projects that shaped me came early, at university: as a developer in the ATLAS research group, I built an educational game to help children with dyslexia train auditory perception and memory. Seeing software make a real difference for those kids is what still drives how I work today: understand the real problem first, then build something people actually use.

## 🚀 What I do

- **Product engineering:** from Figma layouts to front-end, back-end and deployment, as founder of OnlineTec Solutions, where I've delivered 30+ websites and 5 SaaS systems
- **Salesforce Marketing Cloud:** 200+ transactional journey emails, 50+ reusable components and 20+ templates at enterprise scale
- **AI-assisted development:** using AI to ship faster while keeping code review and testing in my hands
